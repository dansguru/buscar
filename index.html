Frequently asked questions about BUSCAR

How do I request a bus booking?

Search for your route and travel date, review the options, then contact BUSCAR to confirm the trip, fare, and next steps.

Are the displayed schedules and fares current?

The website currently displays sample schedules, fares, and seat availability. Confirm current details with BUSCAR before paying.

Can I pay online on this website?

No. This website does not process online payments. Confirm payment instructions directly with BUSCAR and do not pay through an unverified link.
